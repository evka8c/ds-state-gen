Scored on the 38 states this page needs, by what happens to the user. A blocker costs 20 points (12 if half-built), misleading 10 (6), a nuisance 4 (2). App context: unsaved work: partly; poor connectivity normal: no; stakes: legally binding e-signatures sent to the public; accessibility obligations.

Summary

On new envelopes, a recipient who uses a keyboard or a screen reader cannot sign at all: the fields are drawn on a canvas that only answers to a mouse or finger. Of the 38 states this page needs, 3 block signing outright and 3 mislead, while offline and slow loading turn out to be nuisances that a refresh fixes. Three fixes take the score from 22 to 74.
